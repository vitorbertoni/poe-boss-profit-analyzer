How this is calculated

Sample. Every two hours a collector reads the newest instant-buyout warrant listings on the official trade site (all prices, then at least 1 and 10 divines so expensive warrants are not drowned out), plus one search per mercenary skill and support in rotation, Gilded supports first. Only listings indexed in the last 7 days count.

Prices. Converted to divines with poe.ninja rates when the listing was read. Each seller counts once per price, so someone listing fifty warrants at the same price is one observation, not fifty. "Median price" is the middle asking price of warrants that have that class, skill or support; "typical range" is the 25th–75th percentile.

vs. any warrant. The median for the feature divided by the median of all warrants. It shows what moves the price, not the price of one feature on its own: an expensive support usually sits on an expensive class too.

Listed now. How many warrants with that skill or support the trade site reported at the last check: low supply and a high median is what to look for. These are asking prices, not sales — check the trade site before you price your own.
