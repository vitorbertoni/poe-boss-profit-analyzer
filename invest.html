How this is calculated

Daily price dumps from poe.ninja for the last seven finished softcore challenge leagues (loading…). Day 0 is the first day with prices. Low-confidence points are dropped and each series is smoothed with a 3-day rolling median.

Return = price in divine on the sell day ÷ price in divine on the buy day − 1, worked out per league. The table shows the median across leagues, how many leagues the item went up in, and the worst and best league. Only items with data in at least 4 leagues are listed, so newer items have a shorter history.

Past leagues are not a promise about the next one: patches change how items drop, and liquidity matters (on day 2 few people are selling endgame items). Trade time and the cost of tying up your currency are not included.
