How this is calculated

Combos. poe.ninja builds show how many characters allocate each cluster notable. Two notables that can roll on the same enchant and appear on the same character are counted as a pair. poe.ninja does not say which jewel a notable sits on, so a few pairs may really be on two different jewels; those rarely sell, which keeps them low in the ranking.

Sell price. Official trade site, instant buyout listings only, uncorrupted, 8 passives for Large and 4 for Medium, by item level. "Sells for" is the median of the ten cheapest listings after dropping bait prices.

Cost. White base from poe.ninja plus the expected currency to craft it: Alteration until the magic jewel is worth keeping, Augmentation when it helps, Regal, and Scouring + Transmutation on a miss. Mod weights come from the game files (RePoE); the engine picks the cheapest stopping rule, so it may keep jewels with either target notable. It assumes an Alteration gives two mods half of the time.

Prices move and listings are asking prices: check the trade site before you commit. Profit is per jewel and does not include your time.
